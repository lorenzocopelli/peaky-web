This Privacy Policy describes how Peaky ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application, available on Android and iOS.

Data controller: Lorenzo Copelli.
Contact: peakyapp@proton.me.

1. Information We Collect

Peaky itself does not require you to create an account and does not directly collect personal information such as your name or email address. However, the App relies on third-party services described below, which may collect certain information automatically.

1.1 Advertising Data (Google AdMob)

Peaky displays advertisements through Google AdMob. To serve ads, AdMob and its advertising partners may collect and process:

  • Advertising identifiers: IDFA (Identifier for Advertisers) on iOS and GAID (Google Advertising ID) on Android.
  • Device information (device model, operating system version, language, screen size).
  • Approximate location (derived from IP address).
  • App usage data related to ad interactions (impressions, clicks).
  • IP address.

On iOS, we ask for your permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework before accessing the IDFA. You can allow or deny this request when prompted, and change your choice at any time in your device Settings under Privacy & Security > Tracking.

For users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, we use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), a certified Consent Management Platform (CMP) under the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework (TCF), to request your consent before any personal data is processed for advertising purposes. You can review or change your consent choices at any time via the in-app privacy options.

For users in applicable U.S. states, we support restricted data processing (RDP) signals so that, depending on your choices, only non-personalized ads may be served to you.

You can find more information on how Google uses data from apps that use its services here: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

1.2 Cloud Services

The App connects to third-party cloud infrastructure to download resources used within the App. This does not require you to log in or create an account. The only data technically transmitted in the course of these connections is your IP address, which is inherent to any network request and is not stored or used by us for identification purposes.

The cloud services we rely on for this purpose are:

  • Google Firebase, used to store and deliver app data. Operated by Google LLC.
  • Supabase Storage, used to store and deliver additional app resources through a publicly accessible storage bucket. Operated by Supabase Inc.

1.3 Notifications

The App may request permission to send you local push notifications to encourage continued engagement with the App and to remind you to return for scheduled content review sessions. These notifications are generated and delivered entirely on-device and do not involve the transmission of personal data to us or any third party. You can enable or disable notifications at any time in your device settings.

2. How We Use Information

The limited information described above is used to:

  • Display advertisements, including personalized ads where you have provided consent.
  • Comply with applicable privacy regulations (GDPR, UK GDPR, Swiss FADP, U.S. state privacy laws).
  • Deliver app resources stored in our cloud services.

5. Your Rights

It is important to understand that Peaky itself does not directly store personal data linked to your identity. Most data described in this policy is collected and processed by Google, acting as an independent data controller for its own advertising purposes (not merely as our processor). This means that for data processed by Google (AdMob, Firebase), we are not able to directly access, retrieve, correct, or delete that data on your behalf; you must exercise those rights directly with Google, using the tools and channels described below.

5.1 If you are in the European Economic Area, UK, or Switzerland (GDPR)

You have rights under GDPR (or the UK GDPR / Swiss FADP) including the right to access, correct, delete, and object to the processing of your personal data, and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. In practice, you can exercise these rights as follows:

  • Consent for advertising: withdraw or change your consent at any time via the in-app privacy options (this updates your choices with Google's consent management platform).
  • Data held by Google for advertising: exercise your access, correction, deletion, and objection rights directly with Google via Google's My Account / Data & Privacy tools and Google's own privacy policy, since Google, not us, holds and controls that data.
  • Any data we might hold ourselves (currently none beyond what is described in this policy): contact us at the email address below.
  • Complaints: you may lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority at any time.

5.2 If you are a U.S. resident

Depending on your state of residence, you may have the right to know what personal information is collected and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising. You can exercise the opt-out via the in-app privacy options, where available. Because advertising data is collected and controlled by Google rather than by us directly, requests concerning that data (such as access or deletion) should be directed to Google through the channels above; we will assist and forward any request we receive where we are able to.

5.3 All users

You can reset or limit your advertising identifier (IDFA / GAID) at any time in your device settings, and you can change your ad personalization consent at any time via the in-app privacy options. These device-level and in-app controls are the most direct and effective way to manage how your data is used for advertising, since they take effect immediately without needing to contact us or Google separately.

6. Children's Privacy

Peaky is not directed at children and is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

We do not directly store personal data on our own servers beyond what is necessary to operate the App. Data collected by third-party services (AdMob, Firebase, Supabase) is retained according to each provider's own retention policies, linked above.

8. International Data Transfers

Because we rely on Google services (AdMob, Firebase) and Supabase, your information may be processed in countries outside your own, including the United States. Google provides appropriate safeguards for such transfers, including Standard Contractual Clauses where applicable, as described in its privacy policy. Supabase similarly provides appropriate safeguards as described in its privacy policy linked above.
