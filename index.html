How it works

Spaced repetition algorithm

Every word has its own rhythm. The spaced repetition algorithm learns how difficult each word is for you and brings it back at just the right moment; often at first, then less and less.

A deck for every language

Create custom decks for every language you're studying. Add your own words or explore ready-made decks from the Store.

Smart reminders

Peaky notifies you every day when words are due for review. No noise, only the reminders that actually matter.
